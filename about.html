Hello

The baker behind Silk & Sugar

I'm Suhani. I bake every Silk & Sugar order myself in my home kitchen in Austin, then bring it to your event by hand.

I'm also a student at UT Austin's McCombs School of Business, earning degrees in Management Information Systems and Psychology, with minors in Marketing and Educational Psychology.

To me, baking is basically coding with better snacks. Break the flour-to-butter ratio and nothing works. Get the architecture right, though, and you can play with flavor, color and everything that makes a bake your own.

How I bake

Three rules for every order

  1. I

    A melt and a crunch

    Every cookie is soft and gooey in the middle, with something that melts and something that crunches.

  2. II

    The finest ingredients

    Kerrygold grass-fed butter, Guittard chocolate I chop by hand, and organic ingredients.

  3. III

    Baked for your date

    Your order is baked fresh for your event and delivered by me, so it arrives just as it left my kitchen.

Off the clock

When I'm not baking

I'm watching rom-coms, trying new restaurants and looking for the perfect vanilla latte. I balance it with Pilates and yoga, and there's always a creative project underway.

One of them became Girls Can Be Engineers, Too!, a children's book I wrote for my little sister. It sold 1,000 copies in its first month.
